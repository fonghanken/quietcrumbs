Our Story

Quiet Dedication to Natural, Handcrafted Goodness

At TheQuietCrumbs, we've learned that good sourdough cannot be rushed. In a world of endless deadlines and hectic schedules, our story starts in the quiet moments after nightfall, when the frenzy of the day settles into a slower tempo. In this momentary reprieve, a woman dedicated to improving her crumb spent hours on end studying her sourdough starters. It took more than a few months of experimenting and countless feedback loops to finally decipher the fickle, temperamental and unhurried nature of wild yeast.

Fermented with this quiet dedication, our sourdough focaccia embodies our unwavering promise of quality derived only from the natural passage of time. In a world that often seeks shortcuts to efficiency above all else, let our sourdough focaccia be your reminder that the good things are worth slowing down for.

Our Specialties

Our Sourdough Focaccia

Thick slabs of airy, pillowy dough bathed in olive oil, lovingly dimpled by hand and baked to a deep golden crisp. Select from our signature specialty flavours:

Crowd favourite. Sharp, aged cheddar cheese paired with the popular Lao Gan Ma chilli sauce. For the ones who crave the heat.

Sun-dried Tomato & Olives Focaccia

A Mediterranean take on our classic focaccia. Sweet, tart fruity tomatoes against salty, briny olives - think pizza, but focaccia.

Rosemary Sea Salt Focaccia

Our Queen of classics, generously drizzled with premium cold-pressed olive oil, fresh rosemary, and coarse sea salt. For the days when simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.

Roasted Garlic Focaccia

Fragrant, caramelised, melt-in-the-mouth garlic. You either love it or you hate it. In our experience, it's mostly the former.

Indulgent mozzarella balls melted into the dimpled crust, finished with a luxurious drizzle of aromatic Italian white truffle oil. Complex, umami, intense.

Care & Storage Guide

Naturally fermented and baked fresh, our bakes are best enjoyed on the day of collection.

Days 1 to 2 - Enjoy it fresh

Store in an airtight container and consume within 2 days.

Day 3 onwards - Freeze it

Keep in airtight freezer bags and consume within a month. Thaw before reheating.

The Revival - How to refresh:

Oven 180°C | 5 - 8 mins
Air Fryer 160°C | 3 - 5 mins
*For loaf only - Wrap individual slices before freezing and sprinkle a few drops of water before reheating.
