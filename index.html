Our Story

The Craft of Slow Baking

At The Quiet Crumbs, we believe that bread shouldn't be rushed. Born out of a deep passion for natural fermentation, every single loaf and focaccia starts with our active sourdough culture, wild yeast, and high-quality flour.

We ferment our dough slowly for up to 72 hours. This natural process breaks down gluten and yields a deeper, more complex flavor profile with that signature sourdough tang and caramelized crust. No industrial yeasts, no additives, no shortcuts.
